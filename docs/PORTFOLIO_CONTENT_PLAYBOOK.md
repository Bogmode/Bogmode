# Portfolio content rules

Professional examples stay anonymous. Do not publish employer or ingredient names, internal wiring, screenshots, record counts, licensing terms, or other confidential operational information.

Describe personal responsibility and outcomes in general terms. Do not invent performance metrics, customer testimonials, credentials, or live-service claims. Link independent projects and label concepts accurately.

Credentials must link to a record. Course completion is not professional certification. Expired certifications must not appear as current.

Public social feeds should contain only the owner’s authorized public sources. Use headlines and links for RSS. Load provider embeds only after a visitor chooses to connect. Update the privacy page whenever data practices change.
