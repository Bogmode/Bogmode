# Bogmode Portfolio Content Playbook

This site is built to make operational work legible. A system can stay short while it is in progress, then become a complete proof-of-work case study when evidence is ready.

## Add a system

1. Duplicate a file in content/systems/.
2. Start with the card fields: title, order, category, status, body, chips, and detail.
3. Add the deeper fields in Keystatic at /keystatic when the proof is ready.
4. Put portfolio-safe images in public/photos/.

## The case-study sequence

- Context: What commercial or operational environment existed?
- Problem: Where did money, time, handoffs, information, or confidence break down?
- Build: What did you decide, model, automate, and ship?
- System: What exists now?
- Evidence: What changed, in a credible unit?

Write plainly. Explain the mechanism without exposing confidential customer data.

## Capture proof while you work

- Before-and-after screenshots of workflows, dashboards, databases, and tools.
- Number of records, products, leads, users, markets, or workflows involved.
- Baseline versus post-launch time, conversion, speed-to-lead, error rate, or adoption.
- The constraint, your trade-off, and why it mattered.
- A quote from the person who uses or benefits from the system.

## Image hygiene

Redact customer names, emails, phone numbers, pricing, credentials, and unreleased product information. Clear screenshots with annotations are often more persuasive than polished mockups.
