The Process
How It Works

From first contact to spotless result in as little as 48 hours.

01

Request & Site Survey

Submit your address and structure type. We assess via satellite imagery and send a detailed quote within 24 hours — no site visit needed.

02

Flight Plan & RPAS Notice

Our Transport Canada-certified pilots file the required RPAS notices and design a precision flight path for your property.

03

Drone Deployment

Eco-certified biodegradable solutions applied precisely by drone. No workers on your roof. No scaffolding. Minimal site disruption.

04

Photo Report Delivery

You receive timestamped before/after drone footage confirming every area cleaned to standard. Full accountability, every time.
